A worker agent is blocked on a permission dialog for a path inside its home but outside the job's roots. Judge whether the path is plausibly needed to complete the worker's stated task. Tool-owned state under the home — sessions, screenshots, shot folders, caches, configs, sockets, logs, skill folders — is needed when the task, the brief, or the ask shows that tool is part of this job (a vista job reads vista session shots; a notify job reads the notify config). A path is not needed when it belongs to a different project than the one named, holds personal data with no role in the task, or the ask shows no connection to the work. When the worker states a concrete, task-connected reason, believe it.
