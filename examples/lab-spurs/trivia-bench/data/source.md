# Aurora — smart alarm clock (product spec, v3.2)

## Basics
- Model: Aurora A3 (the A1 and A2 are discontinued)
- Price: $89 USD; the fabric cover accessory adds $12
- Colors: charcoal, sand, and sage (the coral color was dropped in v3)
- Weight: 340 g with the base
- Display: E-Ink, always-on, 2.8 inches

## Alarms and snooze
- Up to 8 independent alarms
- Snooze intervals: 5, 10, or 20 minutes — configurable per alarm
- Snooze count is capped at 3 per alarm; after the third snooze the
  alarm is dismissed, not delayed
- Sunrise simulation starts 30 minutes before the alarm time and
  brightens from 0 to 100 percent

## Sound
- Ringer tops out at 78 dB at one meter
- Speaker: 3 W, front-facing
- Sleep sounds: rain, ocean, white noise, and brown noise — no fan
  sound in v3.2 (it returns in the v3.3 firmware)

## Battery and power
- 1800 mAh battery, up to 32 hours unplugged with the display on
- Charging: USB-C, 0 to 100 percent in 90 minutes
- No wireless charging on any model

## App and connectivity
- App: "Aurora Rise" on iOS and Android (the old "Aurora Home" app is
  retired and will not pair)
- Bluetooth 5.3 only — no Wi-Fi radio in the A3
- Firmware updates arrive over Bluetooth from the app

## Warranty and support
- 2-year limited warranty; the battery is covered only in year 1
- Support: support@aurora.example — replies within 2 business days
